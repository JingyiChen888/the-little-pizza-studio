# Image asset notes

The project uses images generated with the AI image tool ImageGen. These were the visual briefs for the final assets:

1. **Topping atlas:** Transparent background, four columns by two rows, consistent top-down view and soft food illustration style. Mushroom slices, onion rings, basil leaves, pepperoni, halved cherry tomato, green pepper ring, three black olives, and a piece of mozzarella. Each item is isolated and centered, without labels or overlap. The game crops the same atlas cell for both the menu and the pizza, except for olives.
2. **Raw crust:** One centered, unbaked classic pizza crust, seen from above, with a soft raised edge and a flat pale center. Transparent background; no sauce, cheese, or toppings.
3. **Baked crust:** An edit of the raw crust that changes only its baking color and surface texture. Preserve its outline, size, and center position; keep the center empty.
4. **Sauce and cheese textures:** A two-cell transparent atlas. The left cell is an irregular tomato sauce spread; the right is a creamy mozzarella spread. No crust, plate, or text. The same small stamps are repeated for the player's marks and for filling gaps after each step.
5. **Pesto and white sauce textures:** A companion two-cell transparent atlas generated with the original sauce-and-cheese atlas as a style and layout reference. The left cell is finely flecked basil pesto; the right cell is smooth, swirled garlic-Parmesan white sauce. Both are repeated with the same brush size and spacing as the tomato stamp, during painting and automatic completion.
6. **Single olive:** A separate transparent black olive ring sprite, generated with the atlas as a style reference. The same sprite is shown in the menu and placed one at a time on the pizza.
